# Biology → robot (V1)

| Concept | Biology | Robot |
|---------|---------|--------|
| Fear | Present threat | High LIDAR risk / near-collision raises `fear_level` |
| Residual caution | Caution after threat | Slow decay when the person steps away |
| Learning | When to defend | Policy / reward |
| Freezing | Stop | `freeze` (wheels 0) |
| Social signal | Be noticed | `catch_attention` |

Refs: Tovote et al. 2015; LeDoux 2014; Maroto-Gómez et al. 2024; Fear HRI protocol.
